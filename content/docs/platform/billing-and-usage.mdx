Billing is organization-scoped. Switch to the intended organization before
changing a billing profile, starting checkout, or managing a subscription.

## Billing profile and regional prices

The Billing page records the buyer type, country, postal address, billing email,
and optional tax identifiers and location evidence. Sensitive address and tax
fields are encrypted and are write-only after submission.

Fabort resolves an immutable published price for the billing country, configured
region group, or global fallback. Currency amounts are stored and transported as
integer minor-unit strings. A published price is never changed by live foreign
exchange rates, and an active subscription keeps its price version, country,
currency, and provider until an explicit change.

## Quotes and checkout

Each eligible payment provider returns a separately frozen, short-lived option.
An option shows the provider, legal seller, payment methods, currency, subtotal,
tax behavior, tax, and total. Provider eligibility is determined by published
country policy, provider capabilities, tax coverage, and merchant approval; the
browser cannot select an arbitrary provider.

- Paddle is the merchant of record. Paddle's localized price, tax result, legal
  seller, and buyer invoice or receipt are authoritative.
- Razorpay is a payment gateway. Fabort calculates tax and issues the invoice and
  any credit notes under the applicable published seller registration.

Checkout may redirect to a hosted page, initialize a provider SDK using public
data, remain pending, or already be complete. Card and bank credentials never
pass through Fabort. A browser redirect never activates service: only a verified
provider webhook or authenticated provider lookup can finalize payment and move
the organization onto the purchased plan. A plan purchase covers one billing
period plus a short grace period; when it lapses the organization returns to
the default plan. Buy the plan again before then to extend it: the days left on
the earlier payment are kept.

## Subscriptions and history

Billing history lists payments, invoices, and credit notes. A
provider change for a recurring subscription creates replacement checkout for
renewal rather than silently moving the existing subscription. Pending and failed
provider operations remain visible and can be reconciled by Finance.

Gateway invoices and credit notes are immutable, sequentially numbered documents
generated from versioned templates and archived with a content hash. Paddle
documents shown by Fabort are accounting references and do not replace Paddle's
buyer tax invoice.

## Plans and limits

Every organization is on exactly one plan. A plan sets every limit that can
grow: projects, members, CPU, memory, disk, replicas, workloads per engine,
public endpoints, builds per day, secrets and secret versions, snapshots and
backups, log volume per day, log and metric retention, audit retention, and
more. A limit is either a fixed amount, unlimited, or not included at all.

Bark enforces limits on the server. Requests that would go past a limit are
refused with a `quota.*` error that names the limit. If usage ends up above a
limit, for example after a plan change, the newest workloads above it are
stopped. They restart on their own once they fit again, either because the
plan grew or because other workloads were removed. Retention limits delete
older logs, metrics and audit records.

Operators can also freeze a plan or one organization's plan, which stops
anything from growing, or suspend it, which stops every workload. They can
close signups or switch off individual capabilities across the platform. The
Usage page shows any of these restrictions that are in effect.

Owners with permission can split project-allocatable limits, such as CPU or
memory, between projects from Project Settings. An allocation only narrows the
organization's limit.

## Usage

The Usage page shows the organization's plan and how much of each limit is
used, with warnings near and over a limit. It also shows resources by project,
the workloads holding CPU, memory and disk, and any workloads stopped for being
over a limit. For a chosen month it adds metered consumption by day and by
project, along with today's log volume and the current log and metric
retention. Metered consumption is only shown where usage rollups are enabled
for the deployment; otherwise the page says the data is unavailable.

CockroachDB is authoritative for commerce and money. ScyllaDB holds plans,
assignments and limits. ClickHouse holds telemetry and metering rollups.

## Finance controls

Operator Finance manages products, effective-dated regional price books, tax
registrations and policy tests, provider availability and credentials,
reconciliation, refunds, disputes, journals, and failed operations. Sensitive
actions require their own permission. Credential rotation and controlled repair
commands require recent step-up authentication and append-only audit evidence.

Gateway sales fail closed until Finance and legal have published complete country
configuration. India remains disabled until seller GST details and reviewed tax
test vectors are published.
